Currently:

Pools is our best-formulated hypothesis for resilience and regeneration finance. We are socializing it with a wide network of collaborators, prospective project partners, and investors. The near-term goal is to firm up the next cycle of implementation and validation end to end, and to settle the network of implementation partners who will give us the sharpest validation feedback.

Also:

We are formalizing covenants of collaboration with three grassroots and Indigenous communities, as part of ongoing research into bottom-up institutional development across Waka and the Institutional Development Kit. In parallel we are establishing a trust in Aotearoa to develop ecological institutions at Moringaehe Reserve in Ahipara and at several candidate sites, with Austin Wade Smith as a trustee, under the oversight of Ngā Hapū o Ahipara, a confederation of hapū in Te Hiku o te Ika, the Far North of Aotearoa New Zealand. This work is the groundwork for a write-up in early 2027.
